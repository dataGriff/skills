# Notes for the CatalogHub review

Context gathered ahead of the quarterly spend review. Leadership wants
to know why we are still paying for CatalogHub and whether to renew.

- CatalogHub (a data catalog SaaS) was adopted 18 months ago on the
  platform team's proposal. Licence: £40k/year, renewal due next
  quarter.
- The original pitch promised a "single source of truth for data
  discovery" so analysts could find and understand datasets without
  waiting on the data platform team.
- Usage stats from the vendor dashboard: 14 monthly active users out of
  roughly 300 data staff. Most sessions last under a minute.
- In practice, teams still find data the way they always have: they
  email the data platform team or ask in the #data-help Slack channel.
  The data platform team answers around 25 such requests a week, same
  as before CatalogHub.
- Dataset documentation is still written and maintained in Confluence,
  "because that's where it's always been". Some teams copy bits of it
  into CatalogHub, most don't.
- Populating and updating CatalogHub entries is manual and optional.
  Nobody was asked to stop doing anything, or to start doing anything
  differently, when it launched. There was a launch email and a lunch
  and learn.
- The platform team maintains the CatalogHub integration and spends
  roughly a day a month on it.
